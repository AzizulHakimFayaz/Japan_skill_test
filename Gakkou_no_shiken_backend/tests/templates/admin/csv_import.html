Instructions for Google Sheets & Excel Users:
  1. Download the Sample CSV Template using the top right button.
  2. Open it in Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel.
  3. Fill in your questions across the 4 sections: script_vocab, conversation, listening, or reading.
  4. AI Audio Generation: Add an audio_script column with dialogues like [Nanami], [田中さん、今週の日曜日に...], [Keita], [日曜日ですね。...] to automatically generate multi-speaker Japanese audio!
  5. Use the group_title column (e.g., "Reading Passage 1") to group multiple questions under the same reading passage or audio clip!
  6. Set correct_option to 1, 2, 3, or 4 matching the correct answer choice.
  7. In Google Sheets: Click File > Download > Comma-separated values (.csv).
  8. Select your target Test below and upload the CSV file!
